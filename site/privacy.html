Privacy & cookies

Last updated 25 October 2026.

This policy explains what information ninefoldvision.online (“the site”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The site is run by NineFoldVision (“we”, “us”). The short version: you can read, browse and watch here without an account, we don’t use advertising or analytics, we don’t sell your information, and we don’t use tracking cookies.

1. Who to contact

For any privacy question or request, contact us at our Discord server. We reply within 30 days.

2. Cookies and storage on your device

We don’t use cookies for advertising, analytics or tracking. The site stores a few small items in your browser’s local storage so that features you use keep working. These are strictly necessary for the feature you asked for, so they don’t need your consent, and they never leave your device unless you sign in.

Our host, Cloudflare, may set a short-lived security cookie (such as __cf_bm) to protect the site from bots and attacks. It contains no information about who you are. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser’s settings.

3. Information we handle

Browsing the site

When you visit, your browser sends technical information (IP address, browser type, the page requested) to our host, Cloudflare, which uses it to deliver the page and to keep the site secure. We don’t combine it with anything else or use it to identify you. Fonts and images are served from our own site.

Ninefold account (optional)

If you sign in with Discord or Google, we receive your account ID and email address from that provider, and store your account data with our database provider, Supabase: your username, profile settings (title, icon, colour), trophy showcase, the players you follow, and the progress and stats our games record for you (for example your level, highest stage or leaderboard placements). Your username, profile and trophies are public, so other players can find you. We use this information only to run your account across our games. When you sign in, or open the player pages (profiles and player search), the site loads Supabase’s library from the jsDelivr network and reads public profile and leaderboard data from Supabase, so both receive your IP address.

Email newsletter (optional)

If you sign up for “new chapters and updates”, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, Buttondown, which sends a confirmation email first. We use it only to send those emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes you from the list.

Videos

The Watch page shows YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. When you do, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may set cookies, under Google’s privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Links to our games, Discord, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Reddit take you to sites with their own privacy policies. We don’t control them.

4. Services we use

  • Cloudflare (hosting and security): privacy policy
  • Supabase (Ninefold accounts and public player pages): privacy policy
  • Discord and Google (sign-in, only if you choose them): Discord, Google
  • jsDelivr (delivers the account library, only on sign-in and player pages): privacy policy
  • Buttondown (newsletter, only if you sign up): privacy policy
  • YouTube (videos, only after you press play): privacy policy

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. They do so under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Why we’re allowed to use it

If you’re in the EU, the UK or a similar jurisdiction, our legal bases are: contract for running your account; consent for the newsletter and for loading YouTube videos (you can withdraw it any time); and legitimate interests for keeping the site secure.

6. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Our host keeps security logs only briefly. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to withdraw consent. Contact us (section 1) and we’ll help. In some places, including California, you also have the right to know what we collect and not to be discriminated against for using your rights. We don’t sell or share personal information for advertising. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Anyone can read and browse the site without an account. Ninefold accounts and the newsletter are not meant for children under 13. In the EU and UK, children under 16 (or the lower age set by their country) need a parent or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we’ll also post it on the Updates page.
